2What is learning, really?

We say “learning” all the time. But what does it really mean?

Think of learning to ride a cycle. You wobbled, fell and tried again, and years later you can still ride. That's learning: a change in what you can do, and it lasts. It comes from experience, from doing it yourself or watching someone else.

Psychologists call learning a lasting change in what we can do, and it comes from practice and experience. So the question isn't only “what did they cover?” but “what changed, and did it stay?” It's true for you too. Anything you can do now that you couldn't before, you learnt.

Psychology defines learning as a relatively permanent change in behaviour that comes from practice and experience, whether direct or watched. Covering a topic isn't the change itself. Look for what learners can still do, use or explain weeks later.
